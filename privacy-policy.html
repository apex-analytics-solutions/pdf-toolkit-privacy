Privacy Policy for PDF Toolkit

Last updated: October 6, 2026

PDF Toolkit is an offline Android application designed to provide simple PDF-related utilities such as viewing and creating PDF documents from images.

1. Information We Collect

PDF Toolkit does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the application's core features.

The application is designed to process supported files locally on the user's device. We do not intentionally collect, store, or transmit users' personal documents to our servers.

2. Files and Documents

PDF Toolkit may require access to files or media selected by the user in order to provide features such as viewing PDF documents or creating PDF files from images.

Files selected by the user are processed for the requested functionality. The application does not intentionally upload these files to a remote server.

3. Internet Access

PDF Toolkit is designed primarily as an offline utility. The core PDF processing features do not require users to create an online account or upload their documents to a server.

4. Third-Party Services

PDF Toolkit does not intentionally share users' personal information or documents with third-party services for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Advertising

PDF Toolkit does not currently display advertisements.

6. Children's Privacy

PDF Toolkit does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the application does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information, it is designed to minimize the collection of user information.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the application and its functionality. Users should also keep their Android device protected with appropriate device security measures.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or PDF Toolkit, you may contact us through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing or within the application.

10. Acceptance of This Policy

By using PDF Toolkit, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
